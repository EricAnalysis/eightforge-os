## Step 1: Answer the seven questions

Use the text in `01-intake-answers.json` exactly as written.

| On screen | Field |
|---|---|
| What workflow costs your team the most time or creates the most friction? | `workflowDescription` |
| What information, documents, or systems are involved? | `informationOrSystems` |
| What does someone have to check, compare, or verify? | `manualChecks` |

| What happens when something is missing, wrong, or unclear? | `exceptions` |
| What decisions require a human? | `humanDecisions` |
| What does a successful outcome look like? | `successOutcome` |

You can read the plan and add supporting documents. An authorized reviewer records resolutions, waivers, and the plan decision. If the reviewer asks you something, answer from your role.
